Why Karjat

A town on the way up, not just a stop on the way through.

Beyond the hills and the commute, Karjat is quietly becoming a place with its own economy — rail, road and jobs, all moving in the same direction.

230+

Best Train Connectivity

230+ express trains connecting Mumbai & Pune halt at Karjat daily, plus 94 local trains to Kalyan, Thane, Dadar, CST & Khopoli.

28 min

Built on Rail, Not Just Road

NH4 and the new State Highway 54 (Panvel–Chakan) cut road travel time toward Panvel, and the proposed Karjat–Panvel rail line trims another 28 minutes off the trip to CSTM.

9+

A Town That's Also Hiring

Amazon, Flipkart, Uber, Ola, Swiggy, Zomato, Rapido, Jiomart and Radisson Hotels frequently hire locally — Karjat isn't just a commute away from work, it's becoming a place to work.

2-way

Smooth Road Connectivity

Connects Murbad, Kalyanpur and Kasara Ghat on one side, and Khopoli, Khalapur, Neral, Ambarnath, Badlapur and Panvel on the other.
